# Redraw request for Sol (2026-10-28)

From a full review of the live frames (every stage, mood and pose of the 13 ready
species). The fixes that could be made here are done; these need new drawings. Each
item names the frame to match. Sizes are art pixels on the 64-pixel grid (one art
pixel is 4 px in the 256 px frame).

## Needs new art

- **Axolotl adult, all 20 frames** (`adult_<mood>_<1|2>`) and **luna moth adult,
  all 20 frames.** The cut lost the dark outline along much of the silhouette and
  left mid-tone fringe and stray pixels around it, inside the body too; the faces are
  smudged and the "z" and "!" marks are broken. `art/adults/outline_fix.py` has closed
  the outer outline as a stopgap, but the interior noise and faces remain. Match the
  teen's clean one-art-pixel outline and readable face (`teen_content_1`). The raw
  sheets (`~/github/hatch-art/<name>-adult/`) may simply need re-cutting.
- **Deer `calling` and `dirty`, teen and adult (8 frames).** These deer are a
  leaner, smaller animal (about 75% of the body area of the other moods) at full height,
  so the adult reads as a teen. Redraw at the build of `teen_content_1` /
  `adult_content_1`. A uniform upscale would clip the antlers at the top.
- **Kitten `adult_calling` and `adult_dirty` (4 frames).** A leaner, taller cat with
  a lighter, thinner outline than the other adult moods. Match `adult_content_1`.
- **Kitten `baby_calling` and `baby_dirty` (4 frames).** About 80% of the baby's size.
  Match `baby_content_1`.
- **Squirrel `teen_excited_2`.** Clearly smaller than `teen_excited_1`, so the idle
  pulses. Redraw at frame 1's size.

## Smaller things

- **Octopus baby:** `content` and `excited` are a saturated red-orange; every other
  baby mood is pale salmon, so the colour jumps with the mood. One shade for all.
- **Octopus `child_colour_shift_1/2`:** no visible colour change between the frames.
- **Tarantula:** drawn on a 2 px grid, finer than every other species' 4 px; its
  dark face on a dark body makes content, bored and sleepy hard to tell apart.
  Lighter eyes or a face highlight would help.
- **Bat adult:** fuzzier fur, and the outline drops out around the ears and head.
- **Gecko `teen_calling_1/2`:** the "!" reads as a brown blob.
- **`child_mask`** for the deer, luna moth and tree frog: the mask barely shows.

## Done here (2026-10-28)

- Axolotl and luna moth adults: outer outline closed and lone fringe specks
  removed (`art/adults/outline_fix.py`). The silhouette, the feet on y=224 and the
  separate pieces are unchanged.
- Gecko `baby_sick` and `child_sick` (×0.83 and ×0.78) and squirrel `baby_cozy`
  and `baby_sleepy` (×0.82 and ×0.85) shrank to their stage's size with
  `scripts/art/rescale.py`, feet fixed, one factor per pair.

Still open from the 2026-10-27 review: `bird/adult_asleep_2` (sleep marks clipped
at the top) and `deer/teen_sick_1` (ear touching the top). The fixes that review
describes never reached the repo.
