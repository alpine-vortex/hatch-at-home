## 1. Ask for phone calls at the moment they matter

**Problem (checked).** "notify me" is only in the settings sheet: status → gear → "for you"
(`_renderSettings` l.6384). Nothing else on the card or in the welcome (`notify.welcome`)
points to it. A call gives you a 20-minute window to answer (`R.CALL_WINDOW_MINUTES = 20`).
The review called this "gives 20 minutes", but the 20 minutes are a deadline, not a reward.
A missed call adds a care miss (`model._calls` l.1220), and misses pull the upbringing down.
**One more gap the review missed:** phone calls need the Home Assistant app
(`notify.phones` reads `mobile_app` entries). The card can't tell whether the person looking
has it, so a card-only prompt might offer calls to someone who can't get them.

**Proposal.** The server already chooses which question the pet asks at a visit
(`_visit` → `reply.ask`, then `QUESTIONS` and `_ask`). Add one question, `phone`. The server
offers it only when all of these hold:
- the person looking has at least one phone (`notify.phones(hass, user)`);
- they don't get this pet's calls yet (not in `state.notify_users`);
- they haven't been asked before (a new per-person `phone_asked` flag in the people store,
  so the pet asks once per person, house-wide);
- the pet missed a call recently (`recent_misses` has one), or a call is open right now;
- it isn't a shared screen (the card never sends `visit` there) and the pet isn't an egg.

"yes" calls the existing `notify_me` websocket. "not now" only sets the flag. Either answer
sets `phone_asked`, so the pet never asks twice. `forget_me` clears the people store, so a
person who forgets their data may be asked once more. That's fine.

**Slices.**
- **1a (M, tier 2: save + privacy).** Server: `phone_asked` in `people._PERSON_KEYS`; the
  `phone` ask in the visit reply; it marks itself asked when sent. Tests: asked once, never
  without a phone, never when already subscribed, cleared by `forget_me`. Card: a
  `QUESTIONS.phone` entry and its "yes" path (`_setNotify`). Bench: a preset shot of the
  question. card-check: the `ask` section gets the phone question.

**Player lines (DRAFT).**
- Question: "can I call your phone when I need you?"
- Answers: "yes ♥" / "not now"
- After yes: "yay! I'll ring you ♥"
- After not now: "ok! it's in my settings if you change your mind"

### 6.1 The social meter (approved as designed, owner 2026-11-01)

**What it is.** A heart track for the pet's social life, beside the bond hearts on the
status page. The bond hearts show one person and the pet. This one shows the pet and the
world beyond the household. It's the pet's, not a person's, so everyone sees the same thing,
shared screens included.

**What feeds it (S1, S2).** Only things the game already has:
- **A visitor greeted.** A visit counts when someone looks at the card during the visitor's
  window. The server already gets the card's `visit` call, so it can tell. Recommended over
  "every credited visit": visits are credited "seen or not" (visitors-v2 Q1), so counting
  them all would fill the meter with nobody there, about 4 days in 7, whatever the family
  does.
- **An infrared link.** Today the link is card-only: once a day per pair, in `localStorage`
  (`oncePerDay("link-<pair>")`, l.~8632). To count, the card tells the server when a link
  plays (a new websocket command, `virtual_pet/linked`, with the two pets' entity ids). The
  server keeps one per pair per day, however many browsers report it. **This reverses the
  first mate's decision 6 of 2026-10-31** ("no counts between pets: kindred pairs are flavour
  from the day's link only"). That's why it's a question (S2).
- **Gifts: not recommended.** A gift is private to one person (visitors-v2 R3, Q7: "nothing
  for the pet or the bond when a gift is taken"). A pet-level meter that rose when someone
  took a gift would tell everyone at the screen that a gift was taken.

**How it moves (S3).** It's a **rolling week**, not a lifetime total. The server keeps the
last 7 days of social moments per pet and shows a level from 0 to 10 (five hearts, in
halves, like the bond). Proposed: a greeted visitor is 2 halves, and a link is 1 half per
neighbour per day; the level is capped at 10. It falls off as old days drop out of the week,
so it never says "you failed". There's no penalty, no "lonely" state, and no effect on
happiness or care (S4).
- A one-pet home has no links, so visitors alone fill it to about 4 hearts in a good week. A
  home with pets that link daily fills it. The question is whether that's fair (S5).

**How it fits "no count" (visitors-v2 Q5, top10 Q6).** It shows a *level of the last week*,
drawn as hearts, never a number or a lifetime tally. Nothing lists which visitors or
neighbours, and nothing ranks pets or people. The pet's journal doesn't record it. It's
closer to the "happy" hearts than to a collection count.

**Saved?** Yes, so it's **tier 2**. A new `PetState.social`: `{day: {"visitor": bool, "links":
[device ids]}}` for the last 7 days (dates and the household's own device ids only; no
person, no user id). The device ids are the other pets in the house, which is already known.
It's pruned on each tick, validated on load (bad entries dropped), redacted in diagnostics,
and kept across generations? (S6; recommended: **no**, a new egg starts its own social life.)
Exposed to the card as `social_level` (0-10) on `sensor.life_stage`, unrecorded. The raw days
never leave the save.

**Where it shows.** A status row "friends" with five hearts, right under "you two". It's hidden
at 0, so a pet with no social life yet doesn't show an empty track. Zero art: it reuses the
`stat_heart_*` images. One pet line the first time in a week it reaches 5 hearts (DRAFT).

**Player lines (DRAFT).**
- Row label: "friends"
- At full hearts, once a week: "so many friends this week! ♥"
- Aria: "friends this week: <n> of 10"
