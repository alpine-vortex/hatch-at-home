# Audit, 2026-10-26 (after the shelf card, v0.3.0)

Two independent reviews: an adversarial pass on the integration logic and a
usability pass on the card and setup, run against the real card in the test HA.
Findings were reproduced by running code. Items marked ✔ were also re-checked by hand.

## Must fix (users will hit these)

| # | Where | Problem | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 ✔ | model `_start_call` | Answering a call with a small top-up (a snack on a hunger call, praise on a happiness call) leaves the reason latched until the stat passes 40. The pet then never calls again: in testing, hunger reached 0 in 12 h with no call and no miss. | Latch on a **miss**, not when the call starts. |
| 2 ✔ | card | The faded neighbouring device is clickable: tapping its edge feeds the other pet. | Make non-current slides `inert`. A tap on one moves the shelf to it. |
| 3 ✔ | card | The resize observer stops after HA re-attaches the card, so the layout never adapts to rotation or resizing and the device gets cut off. | Start observing again in `connectedCallback`. |
| 4 ✔ | card | The first care press on a new pet gives no feedback; the first log entry is skipped. | Initialise `lastLogAt` on first render. |
| 5 | card | During the egg stage every button looks active but does nothing, and there's no hint about hatching. | Dim care buttons on the egg. Show "hatching in ~N min". |
| 6 | card | After a swipe, the next tap on the screen is swallowed. | Clear the swipe flag on the next `pointerdown`. |
| 7 | card | Keyboard and screen reader: the status screen can't be focused, focus moves through the hidden device's buttons, dots lose focus on every update, the NIGHT button is announced as "Lights out", and the focus ring is invisible. | Make the screen a `role=button`, mark off-screen slides `inert`, update dots in place, match aria-labels to the visible labels, and add a `:focus-visible` ring. |
| 8 | card | Contrast: button labels 4.43:1, terrarium subtitle 4.02:1, inactive dots 1.5–1.9:1, disabled labels 1.85:1. Pixelify "C" reads as "O" at 11 px with wide letter-spacing. | Darker ink, stronger dots, labels at 12 px with less spacing. |
| 9 | setup | The setup screen promises bedtime options that don't exist until phase 3. | Remove the sentence for now. |

## Should fix (edge cases)

| # | Where | Problem | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 10 ✔ | model | The 24 h cap is never used. A big wall-clock jump while HA runs (NTP sync, VM resume) is lived at full rate: 60 days produced 62 misses and a sick adult. | In `advance`, freeze any gap longer than a few ticks, as with downtime. |
| 11 | model | Lights-out pressed during the egg is stored but ignored after hatching. A lights-out nap shortly before bedtime ends at 95 energy, and the pet stays up. | Apply lights-out on hatch. A nap that runs into bedtime becomes the night's sleep. |
| 12 | model | A DST fall-back with a bedtime after 01:00 wakes the pet for an hour. Latent until bedtime is configurable. | Fix the wake time as a timestamp when the pet falls asleep. |
| 13 | coordinator | An empty world roster falls back to another world's species (a terrarium hatches a bird). With no ready species at all, every tick crashes. | Keep the roster world-scoped. If it's empty, keep the egg unhatched and log a warning. |
| 14 | model | Old saves with an unknown call reason or latch crash every tick. | Sanitise in `from_dict`. |
| 15 | card | Unavailable stats show NaN, and NIGHT stays enabled when the switch is unavailable. | Show "–" and disable. |
| 16 | card / species | A `species.json` missing `stages` or `moods` blanks the whole shelf. | Validate in `species.py`. Wrap each slide's render. |
| 17 | card | A device rename doesn't refresh the title. "N min ago" goes stale. A config that matches nothing says "add the integration". | Include device names in the change check. Refresh the status page when it opens. Give a distinct message. |
| 18 | model | Fake calls reveal themselves (`reason: fake` on the event, a distinctive speech line). | Hide the reason on the event. Keep the cheeky line, since it's a fair hint in the original toys too. |

## Polish

- **Narrow widths:** set `min_columns: 6`; at very narrow widths, use smaller button caps.
- **Motion:** honour `prefers-reduced-motion` and pause animation while the card is off-screen.
- **Touch hint:** add a small visible "tap for status" hint (tooltips don't exist on touch).
- **Care log:** show refused actions as refused, not as if they worked.
- **Translations:** card text is English-only. Phase 5 or later.
- **Smaller fixes:** the fast-forward `hours` minimum differs between the schema and `services.yaml`; the first real wake after a fast-forward isn't flagged as simulated; an old egg ignores its phase-2 species (no effect today); a duplicate pet name is allowed, giving `pip_2`.

## Open question

The reviewer suggested registering the card JS automatically with `add_extra_js_url`. That would remove the Advanced-mode, resource and `?v=` steps. The original brief said not to auto-register the resource, so this is the owner's call.

## Solid

- Downtime freezing, step-size invariance and per-hour rates.
- Persistence, delete-on-remove, stable unique IDs, recorder hygiene.
- XSS-safe rendering, registry-based pet discovery, an offline font, and charming feedback.
